How Data Moves

The core flow turns people and documents into property intelligence without losing source evidence.

1. Create or match property A home exists in properties with address rows in property_addresses.
2. Connect person property_people records role, permission and verification for that user.
3. Link document Existing userDocuments records are attached through property_documents.
4. Preserve evidence evidence_sources records source, locator, method and confidence.
5. Store fact property_facts stores the structured claim and its verification state.
